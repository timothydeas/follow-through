## Full description

```
Follow through, in the moment.

FollowThru helps you remember to act on your intentions at the moment that matters — by tying each one to a single, distinctive cue from your own life.

Most reminders just go off at a time of day. FollowThru works the way intentions actually stick: you link what you want to do to a specific trigger — a moment, place, or habit you already have — so the nudge lands exactly when you can act on it.

It's built on a simple, well-studied idea: people follow through far more often when they make a concrete plan that connects a cue to an action — "When ___, I'll ___." FollowThru turns that into a few taps.

HOW IT WORKS
• Set an intention — the thing you want to follow through on.
• Design one cue — a distinctive trigger you'll actually notice.
• Choose when it surfaces — once, daily, or weekly.
• Act in the moment — when the cue fires, a notification brings up your intention. One tap to log it.
• Look back — Progress shows what you've actually followed through on, and the cue that's working best for you.

THREE SIMPLE TABS
• Intentions — your active plans; create new ones; respond in one tap.
• Progress — a look back at the days you followed through, newest first, plus the cue that's working best for you.
• Settings — light/dark theme, notifications, app lock, privacy.

ENCOURAGING BY DESIGN
Building a habit isn't all-or-nothing. Progress only ever shows what you did — the days you followed through, grouped simply by when. There's no streak to break, no chain, no score, and nothing counted against you. A miss just doesn't show up. No guilt, no shaming — just an honest, encouraging picture of what's working.

PRIVATE BY DEFAULT
• No accounts, no sign-in.
• No cloud, no analytics, no tracking — nothing leaves your device.
• Everything you create is stored locally and is never visible to anyone, including us.

Free. No ads. No subscriptions.

Note: because your data stays only on your device, there's no backup — uninstalling or switching phones means it can't be recovered.
```
