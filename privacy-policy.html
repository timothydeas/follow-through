Privacy Policy

Last updated: May 2025

Follow Through ("the App") is a personal reflection and goal follow-through tool developed by Timothy Deas. Your privacy matters. This policy explains what data the App handles and how.

Data Collection

Follow Through does not collect, store, or transmit any personal data to external servers. All reflections, goals, check-ins, and settings are stored locally on your device using Android's Room database.

What Stays on Your Device

Everything you write in Follow Through — your goals, reflections, responses to questions, follow-through records, and streaks — lives only on your device. It is never uploaded, shared, or accessible to anyone, including the developer.

Analytics and Tracking

Follow Through does not use any analytics services, advertising frameworks, or tracking tools. There are no third-party SDKs in the App that collect data.

Notifications

If you enable reminders, notifications are scheduled locally on your device. No notification content or data is sent to any server.

Biometric Authentication

If you choose to enable biometric lock, the App uses your device's built-in biometric authentication. No biometric data is accessed or stored by the App itself — this is handled entirely by your device's operating system.

Data Storage and Deletion

All data is stored locally on your device. If you uninstall the App, all associated data is permanently removed. You can also export or delete your data at any time from within the App.

Children's Privacy

Follow Through does not knowingly collect information from children under 13.

Changes to This Policy

If this policy is updated, the revised version will be posted here with an updated date.

Contact

If you have questions about this privacy policy, you can reach out via the Follow Through GitHub page.
