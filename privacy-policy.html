Privacy Policy

Last updated: June 5, 2026

FollowThru ("the App") is a personal reflection and goal follow-through tool developed by iDeas Inc. Your privacy matters. This policy explains what data the App handles and how.

FollowThru is an Android app.

Data Collection

FollowThru does not collect, store, or transmit any personal data to external servers. All reflections, goals, check-ins, and settings are stored locally on your device using Android's Room database.

The App has no user accounts, sign-in, or authentication to any external service.

What Stays on Your Device

Everything you write in FollowThru — your goals, reflections, responses to questions, follow-through records, and streaks — lives only on your device. It is never uploaded, shared, or accessible to anyone, including the developer.

Analytics and Tracking

FollowThru does not use any analytics services, advertising frameworks, or tracking tools. There are no third-party SDKs in the App that collect data.

Notifications

If you enable reminders, notifications are scheduled locally on your device. No notification content or data is sent to any server.

Biometric Authentication

If you choose to enable biometric lock, the App uses your device's built-in biometric authentication. No biometric data is accessed or stored by the App itself — this is handled entirely by your device's operating system.

Data Storage and Deletion

Your data is stored only on your device, and we can't see it. There is no backup — if you uninstall the app, reset your phone, or switch devices, your data can't be recovered.

Children's Privacy

FollowThru does not knowingly collect information from children under 13.

Changes to This Policy

If this policy is updated, the revised version will be posted here with an updated date.
